# Change one variable at a time

When investigating a failure, hold the surrounding conditions steady and change one variable. A repeated result is easier to interpret than a one-off success after five unrelated changes.

This principle resembles focused practice, but debugging a deterministic program is not identical to learning a human skill. The comparison needs that boundary.
